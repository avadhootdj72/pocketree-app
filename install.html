Good to know

  1. Your data stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. Closing the app or restarting the phone keeps everything.
  2. Deleting the Home Screen icon deletes your data. Make a backup first: More > Backup, or sign in with Google for Drive backups.
  3. Moving from Android or another phone: on the old phone save a backup (.pktr), send it to yourself, then on iPhone choose "I have a backup file" and pick it.
  4. Updates arrive by themselves: a new version shows up the next time you open the app.
  5. Not available on iPhone: reading bank SMS automatically, the Home Screen widget, Face ID unlock (the PIN lock works) and timed reminders.

On Android? Ask for the Play Store invite link instead.
